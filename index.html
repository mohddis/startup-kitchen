Founder support journey

From your home recipe to your first sale

Simple, practical and respectful. You should feel supported, not judged.

  1. Apply

    Share your dish, its category, your recipe idea and the help you need.

  2. Bring raw material

    Bring your own raw material and any special ingredients for the trial.

  3. Kitchen trial

    Cook your dish in the Pista House kitchen with a chef guiding and observing.

  4. Feedback

    Practical notes on taste, consistency, hygiene, portion and presentation.

  5. Business basics

    Guidance on costing, packaging, menu name, your first sale and next step.

  6. Launch

    If the product is strong, plan a small trial sale or tasting under your own brand.

About Pista House

Learn from a Hyderabadi kitchen trusted since 1997

Pista House began in Hyderabad in 1997 and grew into one of the city's best-known food names.

Today it serves customers across India and in the USA, UAE, Oman and Kuwait. The Startup Kitchen opens the same kitchens, chefs and systems to home food founders, so you can learn from a team that has built a food brand at scale.
